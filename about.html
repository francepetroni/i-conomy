About I-Conomy

Innovation. Economy.

A spin-off of Aerospazio Tecnologie, designing DC/DC converters for space since the 1990s.

Who We Are

State-of-the-art DC/DC converters for space applications

I-Conomy S.r.l. designs and manufactures state-of-the-art DC/DC converters for space applications using either qualified components or commercial components (ITAR-FREE) tested under radiation.

The company name stands for Innovation-Economy — the commitment to delivering cutting-edge high-voltage technology at competitive prices, with fast turnaround times and a trusted network of reliable suppliers.

Over 3,000 units delivered with a perfect mission success rate across space, nuclear physics, astrophysics and high reliability applications worldwide.

Francesco Petroni — CEO

Francesco Petroni brings years of intensive collaborations with the most important companies, agencies and research centres in the world, and international space project management experience since the 1990s.

What We Offer

End-to-end high-voltage solutions

  • HV power supplies guaranteed to operate in vacuum conditions
  • Protection against outgassing risks (dangerous discharges and corona effects)
  • Space-use HV cables — shielded and unshielded
  • Standard modules in aluminium boxes with mounting holes
  • Custom HV systems with mechanics, control boards and HV cables
  • Flexible cables instead of soldered pins for superior vibration and shock resistance
