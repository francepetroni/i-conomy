Single Channel HV Modules

HV3 series — off-the-shelf DC/DC converters from ±150 V to ±6000 V for space and high reliability applications.

Detectors supplied & application fields

Our HV modules to date have been adopted to supply:

  • Phototubes
  • Avalanche Photo Diodes — APD
  • Silicon PhotoMultiplier — SiPM
  • Silicon Detectors

for the following applications:

  • High Energy Physics
  • Astrophysics
  • Neutrino Physics
  • Dark Matter Investigation
  • Nuclear Physics
  • Material Science
  • Medical Applications
  • Homeland Security
  • Military Applications
  • Industrial Applications

To increase confidence in the utilised components and to be able to reduce delivery times due to a limited number of stock items but in large quantities, the HV DC/DC converters family shares most of the active components. Those components have been successfully tested for Total Dose up to 30 Krad.
