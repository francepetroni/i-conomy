Company Profile

State-of-the-art DC/DC converters for space applications

I-Conomy S.r.l. is one of the few companies in the world capable to provide a complete range of off-the-shelf High Voltage DC/DC converters for Space and High Reliability applications with state-of-the-art performance and proved flight heritage.

Making a high-voltage power supply is not difficult, but if you want it to work for years in space and in any vacuum conditions and perhaps even be ITAR-free, you should rely on the 30 years of experience of those who have made technical and technological choices that have led to the launch of over 3,000 high-voltage power supplies and some of the world’s largest high-voltage systems with 100% success rates.

Engineering Foundations

Three decades of research, distilled

More than 30 years of extensive research and development conducted in the fields of space and nuclear physics have resulted in defining:

  • Design criteria — derating rules for COTS components, robust topologies selection.
  • Quality level of the soldering process — optimal solder pads design for solderability, inspection rules.
  • Protection from discharges and corona effects — suitable coatings and pottings materials, PCB routing and mechanical 3D finishing.
  • An optimal network of reliable suppliers and collaborators.

Resulting in more than 3,000 flight models delivered on time with 100% success.
